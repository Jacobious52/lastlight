The game was built to WebAssembly and actively played in the Codex browser on the
local macOS host. The opening was traversed with ordinary input: click movement,
pulses, darkness toggle, and memory. A pulse opened the first light membrane;
remaining bright left the next membrane closed; extinguishing allowed passage.
A pulse in Witness revealed its moving organism. The Hollow established a
checkpoint and the northern passage could be explored. Reloading restored the

The later indirect-light chamber was played from an isolated development start.
Direct light did not charge it. Leaving an anchor about 158 units from the centre,
then approaching in darkness, activated the structure. The anchor also held the
nearby Still organism in place. Recalling it while remaining dark allowed the
organism to approach and extinguish the player. Death retained the activated
structure and both abilities. This confirms that abandoning the anchor has a
consequence; the residual structure glow is weaker than deliberate illumination.

The large organism and conclusion were also played from isolated starts. A
pulse revealed the large aperture; remaining illuminated nearby was fatal.
Leaving an anchor and moving in darkness allowed retreat into The Weight.
The final chamber required deliberate extinction, then revealed its geometry,
released the small light and settled into the closing screen. The large-encounter
inspection used pause between browser-control steps so observation time did not
unintentionally become idle time inside the live encounter.

The browser AudioContext was verified as `running` after the player gesture.
Waveform, duration, deterministic synthesis and stereo differences are tested in
code. These checks do not replace human listening on headphones and speakers.

Observed layouts included 1280×720 and 1145×1050. Canvas scaling and click movement
continued to work after the viewport changed. No browser GPU or runtime errors
were observed during these plays. With a single active game, the observed average
frame interval was approximately 16.7 ms on the local M1 Pro; this is a local
measurement, not a performance guarantee for other hardware. Frame time is exposed in a hidden diagnostic
output, not a player HUD; concurrent test tabs can materially affect measurements.

## Reproduce focused browser tests

```sh
./scripts/build-playtest.sh
./scripts/serve.sh
```

Open `http://localhost:8080/playtest/?playtest=16` for the indirect-light puzzle,
`?playtest=19` for the large organism, or `?playtest=ending` for the conclusion.
These starts require the explicit Cargo `playtest` feature. They disable saving
and are excluded from the normal release build. They are testing tools, not
substitutes for playing the opening and complete journey.
